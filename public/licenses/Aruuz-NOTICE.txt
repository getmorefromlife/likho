Likho includes adapted lexical data from Aruuz by Sayed Zeeshan Asghar and contributors.
Repository: https://github.com/sayedzeeshan/Aruuz
Revision: 058fcd6010fb0ef030f79b10466c43aa20b0d4b9
Source: Aruuz.Website/DB/AruuzDB.sql (lexical tables only)
License: GNU General Public License version 2; see Aruuz-GPL-2.0.txt.
Adaptation: lexical tables extracted, normalized, pronunciation divisions compiled,
unsupported records rejected, and original record identifiers retained by Likho (2026).
Reproducible importer: scripts/import-aruuz-lexicon.mjs.
No account, report, contact, poetry-submission, or access-log tables are included.
No upstream engine source code has been copied.

The editable adapted dictionary, metadata, and conversion sources are supplied in
likho-prosody-source.tar.gz next to this notice. The complete original database is
available at the pinned repository URL; its non-lexical tables are not distributed here.
