**Subject:** Likho Urdu Prosody Benchmark — Independent Reviewer Expression of Interest

I am writing on behalf of the Likho project (https://github.com/getmorefromlife/likho). Likho is an offline-first computational studio for Urdu poetry composition and prosody analysis, featuring real-time metre scansion, a source-attributed pronunciation lexicon of 40,000+ words, and a live rhyming tray for ghazal construction.

We are conducting an internal review of a ten-item benchmark pilot (LKH-005) to exercise our annotation schema and review protocol. The pilot uses source-exact public-domain Ghalib verses from Urdu Wikisource, pinned to specific revisions. Every item currently records `goldStatus: pilot-unadjudicated` and `supported: false` — no form is claimed as benchmark-supported.

**Why we are reaching out**

Your public work on Urdu metre [reference: your conference proceeding "Meter and Meaning in Ghalib's Ghazal," 2018, or your Rekhta.org metre annotations] came to our attention, and we believe you would be well positioned to contribute to this review exercise.

**What the review involves**

- You would receive ten source-exact Urdu lines, each pinned to a specific Wikisource revision.
- You would independently determine the metre (if any), word-level scansion, licensed reading rules, and a verdict for each line (admissible / invalid-against-target / invalid-against-all / unresolved / out-of-scope).
- You would record uncertainty codes and elapsed minutes per item.
- You would **not** see the engine's metre assignment, target pattern, distances, actual patterns or word readings at any point.
- You would **not** see the other reviewer's worksheet or any prior benchmark result.
- The total time commitment is approximately 10–30 minutes per item (10 items), including source verification and write-up.
- All work is local; no commit, push or hosted CI is involved at this stage.

**Independence conditions**

If you proceed, you would confirm these conditions directly:

- You are qualified to analyse Urdu prosody and are not the Likho engine author.
- You have not seen the engine's output, suggested readings or prior benchmark result for these items.
- You will record a decision before seeing the other reviewer's worksheet.
- You have no undisclosed financial or authorship interest in the outcome.
- You are willing to report the known selection bias (engine-accepted lines only) with any result.

**What we would need from you**

- A brief confirmation that the above independence conditions can be met.
- Your proposed role: **annotator** (first pass on all ten items) or **adjudicator** (review both annotator worksheets and resolve disagreements).
- Your availability window over the next three weeks (estimated total time: 1.5–5 hours, flexible across your choice of days).
- Any conflicts of interest to disclose (financial interest in Likho, Aruuz/Arouuz lexical database, Ghalib publications, or institutional ties to the engineering team).
