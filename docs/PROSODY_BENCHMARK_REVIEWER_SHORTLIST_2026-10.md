---

## 2. Independence checklist (confirm directly with each candidate)

A person is **independent** for these items when all of the following are confirmed:

- [ ] They are qualified to analyse Urdu prosody (`ilm al-arooz`) and are not the Likho engine author.
- [ ] They have **not** seen the engine's metre assignment, target pattern, scansion, word readings or acceptance classification for any of the ten items.
- [ ] They have **not** seen the other annotator's worksheet (if applicable) or any prior benchmark result for these items.
- [ ] They have no undisclosed financial or authorship interest in the outcome.
- [ ] They will record a decision before seeing the other annotator's worksheet (for annotator role).
- [ ] They are willing to serve as an independent adjudicator (cannot be either annotator for any item they adjudicate).
- [ ] They understand the selection bias (engine-accepted lines only) and will report it with any result.
