## 1. Eight-Point Elite UI/UX & Commercial Upgrade Roadmap

1. **Luxury Rebrand & Progressive-Disclosure Canvas (Immediate — Phase 1):**
   - Rebrand primary identity from *Likho / Qalam-o-Qafiya* to **سخن (Sukhan)**.
   - Replace casual system emojis (`✍️`, `🕊️`, `👑`, `🎙️`) in primary navigation and toolbars with thin, cohesive `1.5px` Lucide/SVG line icons.
   - Adopt progressive disclosure in `ScansionCard.tsx`: keep the writing canvas calm and parchment-clean by default, revealing deep prosody/repair controls on demand.
2. **Pure Language Polish (UR | EN Toggle):**
   - Eliminate awkward bilingual parentheticals (e.g. `کلاسیکی (Classic)`) in favor of pure, museum-grade Urdu with an optional `UR | EN` switcher.
3. **Editorial Surface Hierarchy (Reduce Box-on-Box Borders):**
   - Replace nested `border` boxes with subtle tonal surface elevation (`#faf6ee` canvas vs `#ffffff` sheet), hairline dividers, and generous whitespace.
4. **Self-Hosted Nastaliq Typography:**
   - Bundle `Noto Nastaliq Urdu` / `Gulzar` / licensed Nastaliq `.woff2` locally in `public/fonts/` to eliminate external CDN CORS errors and font flicker.
5. **Cinematic Landing Page, Onboarding & Real Checkout Funnel:**
   - Dedicated `/` marketing showcase (interactive 1-line Taqti hero, silent 9:16 Mushaira Video Reel preview, 3D Diwan book mockup, clear 3-tier pricing) + `/studio` workspace.
   - Automated payment integration (Stripe / LemonSqueezy for global diaspora + JazzCash / Easypaisa / Raast for Pakistan).
6. **DAW-Style Audio & Mushaira Studio Redesign:**
   - 3-zone Pro Studio layout: centered spotlight Teleprompter, bottom Waveform + Transport Dock, and collapsible tabbed DSP/Reel rack.
7. **Cloud Sync & Viral Shareable Poet Pages:**
   - User accounts with real-time cloud sync (`sukhan.ink/@poet/ghazal-1`) featuring playable voice recitation + calligraphy card + *"Composed on Sukhan"* viral attribution.
8. **Mobile Companion Experience:**
   - Mobile-first capture, voice memo recording, synced Diwan reader, and 1-tap WhatsApp 9:16 PDF & Video Reel sharing.
